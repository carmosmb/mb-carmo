I'm a Master of Urban and Community Planning student at Pratt Institute's School of Architecture, where studying has given me a strong grounding in urban design alongside the analytical, advocacy, and policy side of the field. My interests range from municipal finance, comprehensive planning, and preservation. I'm drawn to work that connects all different disciplines:  tech, rigorous quantitative analysis, spatial design/planning, community engagement

Outside of planning, you can find me constantly looking at buildings, studying geography, watching or doing woodworking projects, and riding my bike through the city and on gravel roads that probably weren't meant for bikes.

This Portfolio website is a constant WIP, a place to showcase my work as I progress through my studies and whatever I feel like showing off :-)
