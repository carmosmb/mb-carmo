Rockaway Peninsula Transit Access

Vacant lots within a 0.5-mile walkshed and a 0.5-mile Euclidean buffer of subway stations along the Rockaway Peninsula and Broad Channel. The gap between network-based and straight-line service areas shows which parcels are realistically walkable to transit and where TOD investment is most viable.
